Who we are

A small team of Kampala-based developers, building the online presence local businesses are missing.

The problem we kept seeing
We noticed something strange while exploring Kampala's most-loved businesses — cafés with hundreds of reviews and zero online presence, restaurants only reachable through a Facebook page, shops whose only "website" redirected to spam. Great businesses, invisible online.

Why we started building
We're a small team of software engineering students, and we had the skills to fix exactly this — so we started building. Not as a class project. As real, working demos for real businesses, at no cost, because we believe every good business deserves to be found.

What that means for you
When you work with us, you're not getting a template or a generic freelancer — you're getting a team that builds first, proves it, and only asks to be paid once you've seen the result.
